## Project Goal

Build a local stock research terminal focused on AI, semiconductors, infrastructure, and other high-conviction technology themes.

The app should become a decision tool, not just a table of numbers.

Core question the app should answer:

```text
Is this stock worth deeper research, and why?
```

Long-term workflow:

```text
Watchlist → Data Quality Check → Peer Group → FCF Quality → Valuation Sanity Check → Reverse DCF → Thesis Notes → Next Action
```

### 12. Cash Runway explanation

Cash runway only calculates when FCF is negative.

Formula:

```text
cash_runway = cash_raw / abs(fcf_raw)
```

If FCF is positive, cash runway is not applicable.

Future improvement:

```text
Positive FCF companies should show blank or POSITIVE FCF instead of runway.
```
